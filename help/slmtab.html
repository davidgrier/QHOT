SLM Tab

The SLM tab shows a preview of the hologram that the spatial light modulator (SLM) is displaying. The preview updates each time a new hologram is computed. While the tab is hidden the preview is paused, so it does not slow QHOT down.

The hologram is a picture of phase. The brightness of a pixel is the phase of the light at that point, from black for the least to white for the greatest, over one full wave (2π), in 256 steps.

Looking at the preview

Zoom in and out
Turn the mouse wheel over the preview, or drag with the right mouse button.
Pan
Drag with the left mouse button.
Show the whole hologram again
Double-click the preview.

The SLM window

The SLM itself is a second screen, and QHOT fills that screen with the hologram. If the computer has only one screen, or QHOT was started with -s, the hologram is shown in a small window of fixed size (640 × 480 pixels) on the main screen instead.
