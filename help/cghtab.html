CGH Tab

The CGH tab holds the calibration of the optical train: the numbers that QHOT needs to compute a hologram that puts each trap where you placed it in the camera image. Change a value by clicking on it, typing the new value and pressing Return. The next hologram uses it. If the traps are not where the markers say, check these numbers first, and in particular the position of the optical axis.

QHOT saves the calibration when you quit and restores it when it starts. File → Preferences → Save Settings saves it at once, and Restore Settings goes back to what was saved.

Instrument

wavelength
Vacuum wavelength of the trapping laser [μm]
n_m
Refractive index of the medium
magnification
Magnification of the objective lens
focallength
Focal length of the tube lens [μm]
camerapitch
Center-to-center spacing of the pixels of the camera [μm]
slmpitch
Center-to-center spacing of the pixels of the SLM [μm]
splay
Correction for the spreading of the beam with depth. It has no units: the distance of a trap from the optical axis is scaled by the factor 1/(1 + splay · z), where z is the depth of the trap from the focal plane.

SLM

xs, ys
Position of the optical axis in the plane of the hologram [phixels]. A phixel is a pixel of the SLM.
phis
Tilt of the SLM relative to the optical axis [degrees]
scale
Scale factor of the SLM. The pixels of the SLM have the effective spacing slmpitch / scale at the pupil of the objective.

Camera

xc, yc, zc
Position of the optical axis in the camera image [pixels]. A trap at a z equal to zc is in the focal plane.
thetac
Rotation of the camera relative to the SLM [degrees]
