Uniformity Tab

The Uniformity tab shows how even the illumination is across the video, as you adjust it. The plots follow the video, so they change as you turn a knob or move a mirror, and you can see at once whether the illumination is getting flatter.

The plots

There are two plots, one above the other.

Upper plot
The mean intensity at each position x across the video, averaged over all of the rows. The horizontal axis is x in pixels, and the vertical axis is the intensity.
Lower plot
The mean intensity at each position y down the video, averaged over all of the columns. The horizontal axis is y in pixels.

The plots are of the video that you see in the main window, so they include the filters of the Video tab. They do not include the traps.

Reading the plots

  • When the illumination is uniform, both lines are flat.
  • A slope means that one side of the video is brighter than the other. Tilt or move the source until the line is level.
  • A peak or a dip shows where the beam is brighter or darker than the rest, and its position on the horizontal axis is its position in the video. A beam that is not centered is a peak that is not in the middle.
  • A line that is cut off flat at the top may be a sign that the camera is saturated. Turn down the exposure or the gain, so that the brightest part of the video is below the top of the scale.

Color cameras

A gray camera has one black line in each plot. A color camera has three lines in each plot, red, green and blue, one for each color of the video. When the lines lie on top of each other, the illumination is the same color everywhere. When one line is above the others in part of the plot, that part of the video has a color cast, and it is the color of that line.

Working with the plots

  • The vertical axes scale themselves, so a flat line may fill the plot when the intensity is nearly constant. Read the numbers on the axis to see how large the variation is.
  • Zoom and pan as in any other plot: turn the mouse wheel over the plot, or drag in it. Right-click for the menu of the plot, which can export the data.
  • The tab does not change anything, in the video or in the recording. It only shows the video.
