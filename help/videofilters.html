Display Filters

The display filters process the image that appears on the screen. They do not change the video that the recorder saves.

Each filter is a box with a check mark. Check the box to apply the filter, and uncheck it to turn the filter off. Filters work in order from top to bottom, each on the result of the one above. Drag the ⋮ handle at the left of a filter to move it up or down, and click the × at its right to remove it.

The filters at the start

Color Channel
Shows only the red, green or blue channel of a color image.
Smoothing
Blurs the image. Choose the method (box, Gaussian or median) and the width of the blur in pixels.
Sample and Hold
Records a picture of the background and divides every later frame by it, so that what stays the same disappears and what moves stands out. The order, 1, 2 or 3, sets how many frames are averaged into the background (3, 9 or 27). Click Reset to sample a new background, for example after you move the sample.
Canny
Shows the edges in the image. A pixel is an edge if the change across it is above the high threshold, or above the low threshold and next to an edge.

Adding and exporting filters

Click Add filter… to choose another filter from a list that is arranged by kind. Double-click a name, or select it and click OK. Export… writes a short Python program that applies the filters that are checked, so that you can use the same processing in your own analysis.
