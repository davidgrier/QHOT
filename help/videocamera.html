Video Camera

The camera panel lists the settings of the camera that QHOT is using: for example its exposure time, gain, frame rate and image size. Which settings appear depends on the kind of camera.

Change a setting by editing its value: type a number and press Return, use the arrows of a number box, or choose from a list. The panel shows the value that the camera actually adopted, which can differ from the one that you asked for. A longer exposure, for example, lowers the frame rate. A setting that is gray cannot be changed at the moment.

The panel is disabled while the recorder records or plays a video, so that the camera does not change in the middle of a video.

When the size of the image changes, the screen adjusts to show the whole image.
