Video Recorder

The video recorder records the video from the camera, and plays a recorded video back.

The controls

File
The name of the video file. Click it to choose another file in a file dialog. The same file is recorded to and played from. The name decides the format: .mkv and .avi are lossless video, .mp4 is compressed video, and .h5 is an HDF5 file that also stores the time of every frame.
Record
Starts recording into the file. If the file already exists, the recorder asks whether to replace it. It warns you, and does not record, if the folder cannot be written to or the name does not end in one of the formats above. Click Record again, or Stop, to finish.
Stop
Ends the recording, or the playback.
Rewind, Back, Play/Pause, Forward
Play back the file: go to the first frame, step one frame back, play or pause, and step one frame forward. The slider moves to any frame.
Duration
The number of frames to record. Recording stops by itself when it has recorded this many.
Interval
Records every Nth frame. With an interval of 1 every frame is recorded. The recorded video plays N times faster than the event that it shows.

Below the buttons, the recorder shows the number of the frame that is being recorded or played, out of the total, and the time.

Playing a video

While a video plays, the screen shows the video instead of the live image, and the camera panel is disabled. Click Stop to return to the live image. The recorder does not record while a video plays.

Keyboard shortcuts

Press the R key to start recording, and press it again to stop. Press the S key to stop.
