How to Record Video

  1. Go to the Video tab.
  2. Click the file name and choose where the video goes and what it is called. The ending of the name sets the format (.mkv, .avi, .mp4 or .h5).
  3. Set the Duration, the number of frames to record, and the Interval if you want to record only every Nth frame.
  4. Click Record, or press the R key. If the file exists, confirm that it should be replaced.
  5. Click Stop, or press the S key, to end the recording before the duration is up.

While QHOT records, the camera panel is disabled so that the settings of the camera cannot change in the middle of a video. The recording is the video from the camera, without the filters and the trap markers.

To play the video back, click Play. See Video recorder.

Recording as part of a queue

A queue of tasks can start and stop a recording at exactly the right moment, for example while it moves a trap. A task never asks questions: if the file exists, or cannot be written, the task fails and the queue stops. See the tasks StartRecording, StopRecording and Record in The tasks.
